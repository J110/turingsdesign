02 · our advantage

What makes us unique.

AI-driven gamification

Every reward, nudge, and loop adapts to the individual in real time — gamification that personalizes itself, generates fresh content, and tunes difficulty per user. Static game design can't reach this.

Behavioral algorithms

A research-backed understanding of human nature — behavioral science, systems thinking, ancient wisdom — powers proprietary algorithms that drive effective change. No guilt mechanics. No dark patterns.

Design language

Creative layering that merges technology, digital and physical design, and psychology. Delightful, effortless, efficient. The user never feels the machinery — they just feel it working.

03 · inventions

Strategies that didn't exist.

For each product we had to invent a new behavioral strategy — and each one leaves the people who use it measurably better off.

→

Genesis

A personal AI mentor that learns you.

Not a workout list — a coach. Genesis watches how you train, adapts the plan and the encouragement to you, and keeps adjusting. It's the mentorship that makes change stick, so people actually stay with it.

Dream Valley

Stories engineered to induce sleep.

Most kids' content is built to hold attention; ours is built to release it. Pacing, vocabulary, and tone descend to lower arousal and carry a child into sleep — the narrative structure is the product.

Bolaa

Anonymity that makes people more real.

Drop your identity and the fear goes with it. Low-stakes voice rooms let people say what they actually think and bond over their real selves — not a curated profile.

04 · our thesis

Good behavior, by design.

The engagement loop is the most powerful technology in the world. Most companies use it to steal attention. We point it at outcomes people actually want.

Outcomes over engagement

We measure whether the user actually changed — not time-on-screen.

Own the product

We build our own first. Proof is shipped products with real users, not decks.

Distribution over clients

We build and own our products. Partners help them reach the people who need them.

AI as accelerant
